# Parlez

A personal macOS dictation tool: speak, and the words land where you are typing.

## Language

**Dictation**:
One cycle of the tool: start listening, stop, turn the speech into text, and put that text at the Caret.
_Avoid_: session, job, transcription (for the whole cycle)

**Dictation shortcut**:
The key combination that starts a Dictation on one press and stops it on the next.
_Avoid_: hotkey, keybind, trigger

**Recording**:
The audio captured between the start and the stop of a Dictation.
_Avoid_: clip, buffer, input

**Transcript**:
The text produced from a Recording.
_Avoid_: output, transcription (for the text), result

**Caret**:
The text insertion point in whichever app and field has keyboard focus when the Transcript is inserted.
_Avoid_: cursor, pointer

**Island**:
The black pill that grows out of the notch (or floats at the top centre of a screen without one) to show what the current Dictation is doing.
_Avoid_: HUD, overlay, notch, indicator

**Setup**:
The first-launch window that gets microphone and accessibility permission and downloads and prepares the speech model. Dictation is unavailable until it is complete.
_Avoid_: onboarding, install, first run
